Since We Met Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Since We Met Android app, package app.sincewemet. The app is intended for people aged 18 or older.

Your journal and other local data

Journal entries, names, dates, photos you select, anniversaries, the pet, game progress, and local unlocks are stored on your device. Since We Met has no account system or cloud journal. The app does not send these memories to the developer, Google Ads, or Google Play Billing. You choose where any exported backup or PDF goes. A JSON backup may contain private notes and photos. Importing a backup restores its data on your device. You can remove entries in the app and clear stored app data in Android settings.

Home screen widgets can display selected information outside the app. On This Day displays a photo only if you turn on home screen photos in the widget settings. The app stores small previews on the device for that feature and removes them when you turn it off. Anyone who can view your Android home screen may see what you choose to display.

Optional video ads and privacy choices

When you request a rewarded video, Since We Met uses Google’s User Messaging Platform and Google Mobile Ads. Depending on your settings, region, and consent, Google may process IP address, approximate location, the Android advertising ID or other device identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. The app provides Privacy choices in Settings for consent options where Google makes them available. Ads are voluntary and absent for users who own Unlock everything.

Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play purchases

Google Play handles checkout. The app receives product and purchase-state information and purchase tokens to confirm and restore access. Since We Met does not receive your payment card details. Google retains purchase and payment information according to its own policies.

External links and backups

Opening a source or map link takes you to a third-party service that applies its own privacy policy. Backups and PDFs shared with other people or saved to a third-party provider are handled by the service you choose.
